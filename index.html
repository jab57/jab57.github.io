RegNetAgents + CASCADE: Multi-agent regulatory driver identification using ARACNe-based gene regulatory networks and cascade propagation modeling.

Overview

This system integrates ARACNe-derived gene regulatory networks with cascade propagation modeling and multi-agent reasoning to identify upstream drivers and downstream transcriptional programs in cancer systems.
