RegNetAgents + CASCADE: Multi-stage regulatory driver identification using ARACNe-based gene regulatory networks and cascade propagation modeling.

Overview

This system integrates ARACNe-inferred gene regulatory networks with cascade perturbation modeling and structured multi-stage reasoning to identify upstream drivers, downstream transcriptional programs, and system-level regulatory behavior in cancer.

System Architecture

Gene regulatory networks are inferred using ARACNe from TCGA bulk RNA-seq data, then integrated with curated transcription factor regulons (DoRothEA) and protein interaction priors (STRING). Cascade modeling evaluates signal propagation, and multi-stage reasoning integrates structural and dynamic evidence.
