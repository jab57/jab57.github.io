RegNetAgents + CASCADE: Multi-agent regulatory driver identification using ARACNe-based gene regulatory networks and cascade propagation modeling.

Overview

This system integrates ARACNe-derived gene regulatory networks with cascade propagation modeling and multi-agent reasoning to identify upstream drivers and downstream transcriptional programs in cancer systems.

System Architecture

Regulatory networks are constructed using ARACNe-based inference applied to TCGA bulk RNA-seq data. These networks are integrated with curated transcription factor–target relationships from DoRothEA and protein interaction priors from STRING, and further refined using single-cell regulatory signals to capture context-specific activity.
