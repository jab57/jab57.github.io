RegNetAgents + CASCADE: Multi-agent regulatory driver identification using ARACNe-inferred gene regulatory networks and cascade propagation modeling in BRCA systems.

Overview

This framework integrates ARACNe-derived gene regulatory networks from TCGA BRCA data with curated transcription factor regulons (DoRothEA), protein interaction priors (STRING), and single-cell regulatory signals to infer cascade propagation and identify key regulatory drivers.
