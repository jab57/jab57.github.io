RegNetAgents + CASCADE: Multi-agent regulatory driver identification using ARACNe-derived gene regulatory networks and cascade propagation modeling.

Overview

This framework integrates ARACNe-inferred gene regulatory networks with curated transcription factor priors (DoRothEA), protein interaction networks (STRING), and single-cell regulatory signals to model cascade propagation and identify upstream drivers in cancer systems.
